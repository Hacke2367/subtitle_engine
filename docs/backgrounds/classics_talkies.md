## The idea

A seat in the stalls of an old single-screen hall at night. The projector's beam comes down from
above and behind us through slow smoke onto the screen, over rows of dark heads. The song is the
film; on the last line the show ends ("picture khatam").

## Layers

- **Screen** (x 175-905, y 330-770, its right edge a little farther): a black-and-white film, far
  and out of focus: soft shots of the era (a misty river with a boat, a jharokha window with a
  curtain, hills at dawn, a lamp-lit room, an avenue of trees), never readable.
- **Beam**: a soft fan from a port just above the frame (left of centre) down to the screen's top,
  rays and slow smoke inside; it ends softly above the valance.
- **Stage**: dark velvet side curtains and valance, lit from the screen side.
- **Audience**: five rows of people seen from behind (men, a Gandhi cap, a 70s puff, buns, pallus,
  plaits, children), irregular spacing and empty seats; the far rows sit as silhouettes against the
  screen's lower edge; only the tops of heads catch the screen's light.
- **Seats**: rows of empty velvet seat backs between us and them (the lyrics sit over these, dark
  and calm); the near row and two people at the bottom corners out of focus.
- **House lights** (only at the end): deco uplights throwing soft fans up the side walls, footlights.

## Motion and song reaction

- Smoke drifts through the beam all the time; the film's light shifts with each shot.
- The film starts dim and comes up after ~3 s, so a title card at the top reads.
- Word: the film's light lifts a little.
- Line: a cut; the screen eases into the next shot over half a second.
- Marked word: colour breathes into the film and the beam (warm amber with faded teal shadows) and
  the heads' edges glow a little more, while the hall stays silver; it fades over ~3 s.
- Last line: the beam goes out, the crimson curtain closes across the screen (pleats, lit from
  below by footlights) and warm house lights come up on the walls, the audience still seated.
- Without words (`backdrop --seconds`): a cut every 4 s, the show ends at 85% of the length.

## Known issues

- The seat rows are regular; at a glance the lower half can read as a pattern.
- The beam is a painter's convention (a fan from a port just above the frame), not a booth behind us.
- One mood only (black and white with colour on marked words); an Eastmancolor mood for 70s-90s
  songs is not built (simplicity, H-041).
